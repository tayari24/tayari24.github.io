# Tayari24 – AI Powered Government Exam Preparation App for India

> Smart preparation. Better decisions. Better results.

Tayari24 is an AI-powered learning platform built for students preparing for competitive government examinations in India. Instead of asking students to solve random questions, Tayari24 helps them understand **what to study, when to revise, and where they are losing marks**.

Whether you're preparing for **RPSC RAS, REET, Rajasthan Patwari, Rajasthan Police, CET, LDC**, or other government recruitment exams, Tayari24 provides structured practice, previous year questions, mock tests, study notes, and personalized performance analysis—all in one place.

---

## Why Tayari24?

Most exam preparation apps give everyone the same experience.

Tayari24 doesn't.

Every student has different strengths, weak subjects, and learning speed. Tayari24 continuously analyzes your preparation and recommends the next best action so your study time is spent where it matters most.

Our goal is simple:

> Help students study smarter instead of studying longer.

## Project Philosophy

Tayari24 is designed around one question:

**"What should the student study next?"**

Everything inside the platform—from quizzes to AI reports—is built around helping students improve continuously instead of simply increasing question attempts.
